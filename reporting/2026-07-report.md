## Summary

In July the working group shifted its drafted content into the open. The Threat Modeling
Gap Analysis and the Best Practices Guide first drafts were posted as PRs on GitHub, the
Best Practices workstream held its first meeting, and the Design Patterns Catalog
structure was completed and shared for review. The group agreed to align on the security
and privacy terms in the unified taxonomy spreadsheet before the cross-WG taxonomy
effort takes them up.

## Ecosystem Highlights

- Hsiao-Ying Lin introduced the WG at the ETSI TC SAI standards meeting in Sofia, where
  the group discussed covering AI agents in European AI Act standardization, and shared
  updates from the Machina and RAISE summits in Paris.
- OWASP published the State of Agentic AI Security and Governance report, with WG member
  Bar as a contributor. Formal collaboration with OWASP remains in conversation with the
  TC, and unofficial collaboration continues in the meantime.
- The MCP specification is moving to stateless operation with attestations required,
  addressing rug-pull attacks by signing the called tool.
- Fernando's book launched and is free to share, and he is contributing it to AAIF.
- Alex will speak at AgntCon and MCPCon in Japan on September 10-11.
- Govindaraj identified potential overlap with the FINOS AI governance framework, AI
  reference architecture library, and AI readiness work. Alex is pursuing access through
  AAIF leadership to assess collaboration.

## Blockers and Risks

- General availability is a standing risk. Outside demands on members and leads can
  reduce the time they give the group and affect pace in unpredictable ways.
- Black Hat and DEF CON run the first week of August, and a large share of the
  membership consists of security professionals who will spend that week at the
  conferences. Attendance and workstream pace are expected to dip, including at the
  2026-08-04 meeting, and the group is coordinating on Discord around who is attending
  and a possible in-person meetup.

## Decisions Needed from the TC

- The WG requests a decision on formalizing collaboration with external groups such as
  OWASP, AARM, and MCP ACS. This carries over from the June report. The WG continues
  unofficial collaboration until those relationships are formalized.

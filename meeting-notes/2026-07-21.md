## Topics Discussed

- **Chairs meeting update** — Alex reported that progress across the foundation feels slow to every group and that this WG is moving forward steadily. AAIF leadership is aware of the tooling challenges and is sorting out GitHub write permissions, and financial sector members remain limited to email since neither GitHub nor Google Docs is approved for them.
- **Foundation formation and cross-foundation collaboration** — The foundation is still being built with substantial process and bureaucracy slowing some pieces, and leadership remains committed to moving quickly. Formal collaboration with groups like OWASP is still in conversation with the TC, so the group will continue unofficial collaboration until relationships can be formalized.
- **Taxonomy / Landscape** — Jay described lengthy definitional debates, including constraint versus guardrail versus dependency, and argued the group needs to align on fundamentals before building further. He flagged the security and privacy block of terms in the unified spreadsheet, citing the definition of "session" and the need for session ID to account for session replay attacks, and asked this WG to align on those terms first. Junjie added that the unified spreadsheet holds roughly 100 terms, the taxonomy meeting moved to one hour, and defining "agent" remains unresolved.
- **Threat modeling** — Fernando and Alon were absent. Fernando posted a PR for the original draft in GitHub, Alex shared the link in the meeting chat, and Matthew mirrored it to Discord.
- **Design patterns catalog** — Sagar has the catalog structure in place alongside the charter in Google Docs and shared it with Aditya and Aileen on Discord. He wants the architecture guidance group to review how the two deliverables divide their coverage, and Alex suggested syncing with Fernando and Matthew.
- **Best practices guide** — Matthew reported the workstream's first meeting, which covered introductions and a review of relevant existing projects, including the book Fernando is contributing to AAIF. The homework for the group is to review prior work so everyone speaks the same language.
- **Cross-WG review checklist** — Govindaraj has been pulling details on the other working groups and is blocked on Google Drive access by his work profile. He will send Alex a personal email address over Discord for sharing, and he is working with Alex on a regular meeting cadence.
- **Tooling flexibility** — Junjie raised the challenge of tracking feedback across Google Docs and GitHub, and Alex confirmed workstream leads should choose whatever tools work best for their workstream. Alex offered to pursue access to additional software through AAIF leadership when a tool solves a problem for everyone.
- **Agent archetype** — Tom presented an agent archetype that decomposes the monolithic agent into components (input-output channel, LLM, conversation history, orchestration of tools, prompts, and resources) and maps how protocols such as the OpenAI API, MCP, A2A, and NLIP disaggregate them, giving the group a clear way to reason about attack surfaces like prompt injection and MCP poisoning. Erin asked where skills fit, Jay framed skills as the orchestration-layer "brain" that tells the agent how to use its tools, and Jay proposed adding "skill" to the taxonomy list.
- **AI agent certifications** — Thilaknath asked whether industry certifications exist for AI agents, similar to ISO for traditional software. Govindaraj pointed to existing agent validation work and regulatory controls under review, and Thilaknath will look into it.
- **News You Can Use** — Items included the Hugging Face breach, the MCP spec going stateless with attestations required, and the OWASP State of Agentic AI Security and Governance report, which Bar contributed to and will link in the chat. Matthew shared a workflow that turns a Markdown file into a Google Forms survey via Apps Script, producing a 20-question Likert survey in under five minutes, and Alex announced he will speak at Agent and MCPCon in Japan on September 10-11.
- **Black Hat and DEF CON** — Both conferences start August 1 and overlap this meeting's time slot. Jay will start a Discord thread to coordinate who is attending and whether the group meets in person.

## Decisions

- The working group will align on the security and privacy terms in its section of the unified taxonomy spreadsheet before the taxonomy working group takes them up.
- Workstream leads will choose whichever collaboration tools work best for their workstream, with support available to obtain access to additional software where needed.
- Unofficial collaboration with other foundations and groups such as OWASP continues until the TC formalizes those relationships.

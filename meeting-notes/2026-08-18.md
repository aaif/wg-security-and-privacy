## Topics Discussed

- **Cross-boundary observability framework** — Pavan from the Identity and Trust WG requested feedback on a draft framework for cross-boundary observability in agentic systems. Alex will share the draft in Discord and directed Pavan to engage with Govindaraj's cross-WG review checklist workstream.
- **Cross-WG coordination and running notes** — Govindaraj asked for a common forum with leads from other working groups to map dependencies, noting that multiple groups sometimes claim ownership of overlapping areas. He also flagged that several groups do not capture running notes consistently, which makes it hard to analyze their scope and deliverables from Git.
- **Meeting notes practices** — This WG publishes notes to GitHub shortly after each meeting, and the group agreed other WGs adopting the same practice would ease cross-group analysis. Alex will raise the topic in the chairs Discord channel and share the AI prompt he uses to generate the notes.
- **A2A donation to AAIF** — Govindaraj asked how the recent donation of A2A affects this WG's scope and protocol-related work in other groups, especially given Agent Gateway is also part of AAIF and the industry is looking at gateway patterns for guardrails, observability, and orchestration. Alex will raise the question with Manik.
- **Consolidating protocol workstreams** — Matthew argued against every workgroup spinning up its own workstream for the same topic, noting the Observability WG already merged its protocols and primitives workstreams into one. He proposed a single protocols and primitives effort with participants from Security and Privacy, Accuracy and Reliability, Identity and Trust, and Workflows, drawing in people beyond the usual tracing crowd.
- **Collaboration with external organizations** — There is no official AAIF framework for working with groups like OWASP, and nothing prohibits it. Alex encouraged members to surface collaboration opportunities and offered to facilitate conversations, with the goal of consistent messaging so adopting organizations do not stall on conflicting requirements between foundations.
- **Taxonomy / Landscape** — Junjie reported the first pass of the easier terms is nearly complete, conflicts over duplicate terms across working groups have been resolved, and each WG should now create and review PRs. Katherine and Jautau noted PRs will appear over the coming weeks for group feedback, with results expected by end of month or early next.
- **Best practices** — Fernando is out of office, so review of his book is paused. The workstream reviewed Erin's security best practices guide, which resembles standard code smells from static analyzers, and Erin committed to submitting a PR this week.
- **Cross-WG review checklist** — Govindaraj held two meetings so far, and a timing change caused conflicts, so he will re-run the scheduling poll. The team has started preparing the overlap checklist between groups and found gaps where other WGs' notes and Git content are unclear, so he requested chair contact information to ask groups directly about their scope and deliverables.
- **New members and local chapters** — New member organizations including Visa and Wells Fargo have joined AAIF without representatives in this WG yet, and Alex will ask Manik about outreach to new member orgs. Govindaraj shared that AAIF has local chapters requiring at least three events in six months, is organizing a Dallas chapter, and noted existing chapters in Boston, New York, SF, and Atlanta.
- **News** — OWASP released its updated Top 10 for Agentic systems, and FINOS Labs launched the Open Enterprise Agent Governance and Orchestration project covering a governance and compliance control plane for A2A, MCP, and related protocols, which may overlap with this WG's governance work. The group also discussed OpenAI's Black Hat talk on their sandbox escape incident, observing that the presentation downplayed the sandbox design flaws (inter-agent communication and uncontrolled egress) that enabled the escape and impacted Hugging Face.
- **Conferences** — Several members present at AgentCon / MCPCon in San Jose in October, including Bar, Junjie, Katherine, and Jautau, who is running a workshop on his OpenSSF Secure Agentic Framework MCP skill. Alex speaks in Tokyo, Katherine is considering Amsterdam and floated a side meetup there, and the group discussed organizing a San Jose meetup.

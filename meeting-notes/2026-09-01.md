## Topics Discussed

- **Chairs meeting updates** — Alex reported slow progress on the Linux Foundation's new platform, including a non-public MCP he is trying to get access to. The chairs also surfaced a mismatch in outreach to new member organizations, since the people attending sponsor sync meetings are generally not the people who would join this WG.
- **Amsterdam conference social event** — AAIF is planning a social event (dinner or similar) at the Amsterdam conference, with details to come. This can complement the informal plans this group has discussed for members attending.
- **Monthly reporting** — Alex submitted the WG's monthly report Sunday night, making this group the first to file and keeping it off the late list.
- **Taxonomy / Landscape** — Junjie reported the first pass is complete and each working group is checking in its initial 5-10 terms via PRs, with an initial version targeted for the end of September. WG delegates such as Jautau and Katherine should help define this group's terms and review definitions from other working groups.
- **Security and privacy taxonomy terms PR** — Jautau walked through Katherine's PR updating the taxonomy data with this WG's terms and found them broadly in scope. He questioned the "Privacy Preserving Execution" term, since agents operating on encrypted or protected data also covers sensitive and confidential information beyond privacy data, and Matthew added his review during the meeting. Matthew also noted the Workflows WG is aggressively claiming terms, and this group should reciprocate at review time.
- **Design patterns catalog** — Sagar reported the kill-switch design patterns have been added, covering the different kinds of kill switches and how to incorporate them, with memory-related patterns planned for next week. Aditya and Aline are reviewing the work, and Sagar can relay updates to Alex via Discord when the meeting time is inconvenient for India time zones.
- **Best practices document** — The first draft has been under review for almost a month while Fernando was traveling. He plans to finish all reviews by end of day 2026-09-02, then freeze and merge the first version and build on top of it.
- **Best practices as actionable rules** — Erin is converting the security best practices into rules usable by static analysis tools, which could yield a code-facing deliverable alongside the document. She is also reviewing the Google SDK's security best practices, with Amazon, Microsoft, and other major SDKs to follow for comparison. Govindaraj confirmed this work complements rather than overlaps the checklist workstream.
- **Publishing the first deliverable** — Alex proposed treating the frozen first version as a release: publish it, socialize it across the whole WG for feedback, then promote it publicly as the group's first deliverable. Identity and trust topics are excluded from this version because those best practices belong to the Identity and Trust WG, and public messaging will frame the document around this group's areas of focus.
- **Cross-WG review checklist and outreach** — Govindaraj has identified most working group leaders from Git and is drafting an invitation to a cross-group meeting with an availability poll. Matthew proposed the chairs also ask for five minutes in other working groups' meetings to invite collaboration, and Alex will give the chairs a heads-up in their channel. Coverage already exists for Identity and Trust, Observability, Governance, and Agentic Commerce through members and colleagues, and Matthew has invited contacts from Accuracy and Reliability.
- **Accuracy and Reliability survey** — The Accuracy and Reliability WG has a survey coming out and wants broad involvement, which Matthew flagged as a natural fit for the checklist workstream. Govindaraj is also considering a survey across working groups to capture deliverables and priorities, with the survey tool still an open question given uneven access to Google Forms.
- **LFX portal and automation** — Govindaraj flagged that several groups' meeting notes are not being updated in Git and that the Ask AI feature was removed from the LFX portal. He asked whether an API exists to pull recordings and AI summaries programmatically, and Alex will raise both the API and MCP access with Kieran, the Linux Foundation dev experience lead.
- **A2A joining AAIF** — Fernando asked whether this group can participate in A2A discussions now that it is part of AAIF, and Alex said the answer is generally yes with logistics still being worked out. The chairs also discussed the lifecycle of working groups, since the current groups are effectively permanent while requests for more targeted groups are coming in. Erin shared the A2A Discord invite link with the group.
- **News** — OpenAI decided not to renew its contracts with Cursor following Elon Musk's acquisition of Cursor via SpaceX, NVIDIA is acquiring Hugging Face for $13 billion, and Apple has reportedly been surprised by corporate demand for its machines driven by unified memory enabling local LLM use.
- **Dallas chapter community events** — The Dallas chapter launch event is on 2026-09-26, is at max in-person capacity, and Govindaraj is considering a virtual component with speakers from this group for students and industry practitioners. AAIF provides no funding for banners or speaker mementos, and Alex will probe back channels for sponsorship options.

## Decisions

- The first version of the best practices document will be frozen, merged, and taken through the WG deliverable process: publish, socialize internally for feedback, then promote publicly as the group's first deliverable.
- Identity and trust topics are excluded from the first version of the best practices document, pending ownership discussions with the Identity and Trust WG.
